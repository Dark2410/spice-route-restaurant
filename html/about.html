ABOUT SPICE ROUTE

Our Story

Bringing people together across Baner over the warmth and richness of authentic Indian flavours.

A PASSION FOR HERITAGE

Rooted in Tradition, Crafted for Modern Gatherings

We started Spice Route with a simple idea: to bring people together over authentic Indian food. Our restaurant combines traditional flavours, freshly prepared dishes, and a welcoming atmosphere where families and friends can enjoy memorable meals.

From slow-cooked dum preparations to flame-roasted tandoori specialities, every recipe honours classical regional techniques while maintaining clean, balanced presentation. We believe dining out should feel unhurried, comfortable, and distinctly personal.

01 / HERITAGE

Time-Honoured Traditions

Every curry and marinade begins with fresh, hand-selected whole spices, slow-roasted and ground to maintain authentic regional profiles without shortcuts.

02 / AMBIENCE

A Welcoming Gathering Space

Designed with warm earthy tones, comfortable seating, and attentive hospitality suited for lively family dinners, weekend gatherings, and quiet lunches.

03 / INCLUSIVITY

Thoughtfully Curated Menu

A balanced selection covering rich vegetarian gravies, clay-oven paneer, aromatic dum biryanis, and succulent chicken classics prepared with dedicated kitchen care.
